## FPJ's Batang Quiapo
Genre: Action, Drama, Crime  
Main Actor: Coco Martin

### Overview
- FPJ's *Batang Quiapo* is a Philippine action-drama television series inspired by the 1986 film of the same name.  
- It tells the story of **Tanggol**, a man from Quiapo who faces struggles, temptations, and challenges while trying to change his life for the better.
  
### Cast Highlights
1. **Coco Martin** as Tanggol  
2. **Cherry Pie Picache** as Marites  
3. **Charo Santos-Concio** as Tinding  
4. **Christopher de Leon** as Ramon  
5. **John Estrada** as Rigor

#### Why I Love This Series
- Gritty and powerful storytelling.  
- Strong performances, especially by Coco Martin.  
- Explores social issues in urban Manila.
- Mixes action with heartfelt family drama.  
- Inspires hope and resilience despite struggles.  
- Represents Filipino culture and values.  
- Keeps me excited with unexpected twists and turns.

##### Memorable Themes
- **Family:** The series emphasizes the importance of family ties and respect for elders.  
- **Justice:** It explores the thin line between right and wrong, and how choices define a person.  
- **Resilience:** Shows how Filipinos rise above hardship with courage and determination.  
- **Culture:** Highlights the vibrant life of Quiapo, from its streets to its traditions.

###### Fun Facts
- The series is a tribute to the late Fernando Poe Jr., who starred in the original *Batang Quiapo* film.  
- It is one of the most-watched primetime shows in the Philippines.  
- Coco Martin not only stars in the series but also directs and helps in production.  
- The show blends action, drama, and comedy, making it appealing to a wide audience.
